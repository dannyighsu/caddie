# Engagement Criteria

## Feed Opportunity Filtering

Include posts that match ALL of these:
1. **Recency**: Posted within the last 6 hours
2. **Poster**: Individual (not organization), 1K-100K followers, check original poster not retweeter
3. **Technical depth**: Highly technical content preferred — substantive claims, architecture decisions, real experience
4. **Bold takes**: Strong opinions or contrarian views that invite discussion
5. **Topic relevance**: Within or adjacent to areas of interest

Prioritize:
- Posts with growing engagement but still room for quality replies
- Posters you've engaged with before (relationship building)
- Emerging voices with interesting perspectives

Skip:
- Ads, engagement bait, memes
- Organization/brand accounts (TechCrunch, Google, AWS, etc.)
- Posts that already have 100+ replies (your reply will get buried)
- Content that's just resharing links without commentary

## Engagement Style

When suggesting replies:
- Add genuine value — share experience, ask sharp questions, offer a different angle
- Be conversational and authentic, not corporate
- Reference specific details from the post (shows you read it)
- Keep replies concise — 1-3 sentences is ideal for X
- Questions that invite the poster to elaborate tend to perform well

## Reply Prioritization (for incoming replies to your posts)

Include all replies made directly to you, except:
- Simple acknowledgments ("great post!", "thanks!")
- Emoji-only responses
- Spam or promotional content
- Low-effort responses
